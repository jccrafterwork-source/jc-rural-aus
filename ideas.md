## Chosen approach: Tasmanian Field Ledger

### Design Movement
**Rural Field-Guide Editorialism** — an elevated interpretation of Australian farm field notes, heritage tool catalogues, stamped invoices, and roadside service signage. The visual language respects the provided JC Rural AUS collateral rather than imitating a generic tradesperson template.

### Core Principles
1. **Practical credibility first.** Every section should make the next action obvious: review a service, understand the labour-only basis, or request a quote.
2. **Tactile, not theatrical.** Subtle grain, ink-like rules, worn-edge dividers, and stamp motifs support the rural character without compromising legibility.
3. **Rugged editorial hierarchy.** Large condensed headings carry the directness of hand-painted farm signage, while calm sans-serif body copy remains easy to scan on a phone in the field.
4. **Tasmanian context, confidently stated.** The site foregrounds Tasmania-wide coverage, land-based work, and responsive local service without making unverified claims.

### Color Philosophy
The page should feel like durable equipment against the Tasmanian landscape. **Eucalypt Ink** is the ownable signature color: a dense, unmistakable bush green that conveys trust and field competence. It is paired with charcoal-black for authority, warm unbleached-paper cream for openness, and a restrained wheat-gold accent for important rates and calls to action. Color is functional: dark blocks establish confidence, paper areas provide reading rest, and the gold route signals to contact or quote actions.

### Layout Paradigm
The page follows a **field ledger** rather than a centrally stacked brochure. A narrow vertical service rail and an offset main narrative create a sense of a practical job docket. Sections use intentionally varied widths: a wide image-led hero, a compact credentials strip, alternating service ledgers, then a dark booking panel. On mobile, the ledger resolves into a clear linear sequence with the call action kept within immediate reach.

### Signature Elements
1. **The field stamp:** a circular, distressed-mark treatment derived from the supplied JC Rural AUS logo language, used in the header, hero context badge, and contact panel.
2. **Fence-line rules:** fine horizontal and vertical rules with short tick marks, recalling fencing runs and notebook ledgers.
3. **Brush-ink bands:** imperfect green and charcoal underlines or separators that underline section titles and rates without becoming decorative clutter.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel assured and mechanical, like a well-maintained gate latch. Buttons have a distinct press response, service cards lift only slightly, and the mobile menu reveals cleanly without novelty effects. Contact actions always resolve to a phone, email, or Facebook destination rather than dead-end placeholders.

### Animation
Use restrained 160–280 ms transitions with a firm ease-out curve. Hero copy, field stamp, and first service card may rise in staggered sequence on initial load; subsequent content should rely on subtle transform and opacity transitions only. A scan-like line sweep can animate across divider rules once, but motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never impede reading.

### Typography System
Use **Barlow Condensed** for headline hierarchy and utility labels because it has the confident, vertically compact character of rural signage. Use **DM Sans** for body copy and controls because it remains highly legible at small mobile sizes. Headings are uppercase with tightly controlled tracking; body copy is sentence case and spacious. The handwritten emphasis seen in the source material is translated sparingly through italic Barlow Condensed rather than a difficult-to-read script font.

### Brand Essence
**JC Rural AUS provides hardworking, Tasmania-wide farm contracting support for landholders who want dependable help and straightforward rates.**  
Personality: **Reliable, practical, grounded.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be direct and active; CTAs should tell visitors exactly what will happen next. Microcopy should clarify what is included and avoid inflated promises.

> “Reliable hands when the farm needs moving.”

> “Tell us the job. We’ll help you get it done.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a bold, text-free **circular field stamp** as the visual mark: a charcoal ring, a simplified kelpie head profile, a rural worker silhouette, and a small hand-tool motif in eucalypt green. The adjacent wordmark uses custom-spaced Barlow Condensed uppercase lettering rather than a default logo treatment. The mark should work clearly at favicon scale and prominently in the header.
